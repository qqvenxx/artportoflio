MEMORY

Created for my Intermediate Media Arts class, this short film follows a lyrical character who responds to displacement and loss by imposing strict order on her daily life, turning routine into a form of control. The piece explores how perfectionism can become both a source of stability and a way of managing unresolved trauma. Rather than treating memory as something that keeps her trapped in the past, the film presents it as part of what allows her to move forward. Her perfectionism is not simply a flaw to overcome; instead, it is embraced as a permanent, protective part of who she has become.

Nothing. Interpreted.

Inspired by Kazimir Malevich's The Black Square, this philosophical short film explores the subjectivity of meaning and value. Built around an original poetic voiceover, it relies on isolated objects, carefully composed frames, and atmospheric scenery to construct its visual language. The short film considers how objects and experiences acquire significance through the meanings we assign to them. By examining the relationship between perception and interpretation, it explores whether anything can possess meaning independently of the observer.
