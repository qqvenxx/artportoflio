PORTFOLIO 2026—27

PORTFOLIO  /  04

Poetry

5 POEMS

1 Art in Triviality 2025
Mundanity, hacked apart by the axe, No longer a place for life's calm tracks. Kindness, its wings now torn and frayed, The mirror of existence left in yesterday. They shatter—shatter—my dream in despair, Yet all this grayness fades by the minute’s glare. I’ve seen it all: insects, birds, and hope’s glow, But now, it’s just me—nothing else to show. We lose ourselves in this cruel, harsh tide, No humans remain; they've long since died. All—formless beings in human guise, Wandering deserts beneath endless skies. I was there, in a body’s fleeting shade, Where no bounds, no fears, no pain invade. Are feelings—the thieves of my life’s domain? Take it all away, but let freedom remain! I see the hacked and shattered mundane, In its harsh reality, I remain. My art—my truth, my soul laid bare, And willingly, I drown in it there.
2 Where do I belong? 2026
How do you know where the soul finds its home? How do we claim what was never our own? I adapt to the weather, the shift of the tide, But places don't fill the hollow inside. I don't even know what could occupy space— People? Perhaps, for a moment of grace. But isn't true peace meant for those who can stand With only themselves in a desolate land? It's a struggle to settle, to learn a new street, To soften for souls that I'm destined to meet. For why should I lean on a world that will fade? A ghost in the making, a debt to be paid. If not stolen by force, it will vanish by rust, As time turns our altars to dust. Nothing remains when the cycle is through; The memory bleeds when the absence is true. When it is yours, it is easy to ignore, When it is gone, you are begging for more. Is there a point to the value we give, When we hunger for lives that we'll never live? I try not to touch, to keep distance my friend, To never start things that are destined to end. I'm guarding the void, the cold and the vast, So the silence inside doesn't deepen at last.
3 The Art of Finding 2025
Making something a part of you is not a crime. But for it to break, there is no right time. Is there ever a "right time" for a heart to break? How much of one's spirit can it take? It's hard to lose; you feel you'll never find The things, the soul, or the peace of mind. Deep feelings weave into the very bone, Then the heart shatters—and you are left alone. The climb to find yourself is steep, The pain is only yours to keep. You cannot glue the shards back to how they were, But try, try, and try again, do not deter. Until the cracks align in at least a jagged line, A rough-edged wholeness that is now yours, and fine. All your life you'll lose; get used to the cost, But you are the only thing that can't be truly lost. Drowning in grief, find your own way through, Rediscover the core, find the center of you. We enter this world with hands empty and bare, And leave it alone, with only silence to share. So "the art of losing isn't hard to master", But how will you survive it? That's the disaster.
4 The Path 2026
you can't have everything, they say— yet still i reach for it every day. and when i fall, when i don't try— am i broken, or just… why? my priorities are clearly known: "dream hard—work harder"—set in stone. in my brain, i rest in what i've done, in my heart, i ache for more to come. in my restraint, i watch, observe— don't speak unless i've been conferred. think twice before you cut too deep, bury the wants you'd like to keep. do not what you want, but what is right, resist the wish with all your might. i don't have time—my work takes whole, it eats and echoes through my soul. yet still i say: i'll find a way, to steal back hours from the day. i think twice—no, more than that, until my thoughts compact and formulate. but something now aligns, at last— my wants don't feel so far surpassed. i'm still reaching for everything, and for once, it feels like i might win.
5 Call 2024
I write my thoughts in rhyme—, Will they, burned, carve their way through time? Or better this: to write, then burn, To what end should the pages turn? Who ever said I have the gift? No one! No one gave my soul this lift. Yet deep within, I felt it, clear— I yearn to write, for it draws me near. I write because I crave, because I can't refrain! I care not for your thoughts or your disdain. This is my vision, my words, my voice— To write is my calling and my choice.
